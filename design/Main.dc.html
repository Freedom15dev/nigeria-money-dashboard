What this means

Since January 2025, none of these options kept up with prices. Treasury bills came closest. Dollars did worst over this period because the naira strengthened, but over longer periods the picture changes. Try an earlier start year to see.

How each option is worked out

Savings account and Treasury bills grow each month at the CBN average rate ÷ 12. The fixed deposit rolls over every 12 months at the rate then. Dollars are bought at the starting month's official rate and sold at the latest. "Today's prices" uses a price index built from CBN inflation. Real accounts, taxes and fees differ.
